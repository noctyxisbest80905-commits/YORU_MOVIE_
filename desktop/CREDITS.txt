YORU
Created by Obscura.

Yoru is the desktop application, interface, and packaging built on the
MovieBox TUI project. The original provider engine and terminal app retain
their upstream authorship and licenses; this is not a claim that Obscura
created the original MovieBox TUI engine or third-party services.

Upstream foundation:
MovieBox TUI contributors — https://github.com/mesamirh/MovieBox-Tui
License: MIT OR Apache-2.0. See LICENSE-MIT and LICENSE-APACHE.

Configured third-party streaming providers:
MovieBox; 4KHDHub; Dramachi; CircleFTP (BDIX); DhakaFlix (BDIX).
The 4KHDHub resolver also supports HubCloud and Pixeldrain links.
Additional hosting/CDN servers vary by release and are not owned by Yoru.
BDIX services require a compatible network.

Players and application technologies:
VLC — VideoLAN; mpv — mpv contributors; pywebview; Microsoft WebView2;
Rust and Python communities; PyInstaller; NSIS.
