YORU CUMULATIVE RELEASE
Includes the Yoru/Obscura/provider credits update and all subsequent app changes.
Current Android APKs are v0.4, non-debuggable and signed with the v0.3 filtered
build certificate. Do not follow old v0.3 uninstall advice unless your own installed
build actually has a different certificate. Uninstalling deletes local app data.
Current scope, signing, features and validation are documented in README.md and
ANDROID_OFFLINE_FULLSCREEN.md. Older v0.3 notes are preserved under docs/release-history/.
