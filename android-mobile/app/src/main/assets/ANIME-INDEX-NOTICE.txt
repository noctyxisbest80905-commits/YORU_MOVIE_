Yoru explicit-anime title index: a derivative of "anime-offline-database" by
manami-project. Source: https://github.com/manami-project/anime-offline-database
Snapshot: release 2026-27, lastUpdate 2026-07-04.

Modifications: retained titles and synonyms for records tagged "hentai" or
"pornography" and, separately, harem-classified records. Pictures, thumbnails,
descriptions and all other data were omitted. The primary Sword Art Online record
is excluded from the harem list at the user’s request, not from explicit checks.
Yoru uses these alias lists as offline filters. Harem is not the same as explicit content.

This derivative database is offered under ODbL-1.0 and DbCL-1.0. The full derivative
is included as adult_anime_index.json, and its reproducible extraction script is
included in the source update. See ANIME-INDEX-LICENSE.txt for both licenses.
These database licenses do not change the app's separate source-code licenses.
Neither the dataset authors nor metadata providers endorse or are affiliated with Yoru.

Known limitations: classifications can be wrong or incomplete, aliases can collide,
and newly released or mislabeled explicit content can be missed. This is not a
parental-control certification or a guarantee that any result is appropriate for children.
No user search history or titles are sent to the offline database project at runtime.
